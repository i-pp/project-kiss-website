## Project-AceIS

**Next-generation system software / Research and development vision**

Project-AceIS is guided by the KISS principles of Project-KISS. Its vision emphasizes real-time behavior, predictability, portability, and verifiability, with an intended scope spanning operating-system foundations and compiler technology.

[Explore Project-AceIS →](/products/aceis/)

> This is a vision and a set of development goals. It is not a statement of delivered features, performance guarantees, or a release schedule.

## How philosophy and product relate

Project-KISS defines the engineering principles used to approach a problem. Project-AceIS has a specific research and development scope within that broader philosophy.

Use the [philosophy](/philosophy/) to understand the approach. Use the AceIS page to understand its goals and the limits of the information currently presented here.
