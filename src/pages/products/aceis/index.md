## The vision

Project-AceIS is a research and development initiative for next-generation system software. Its intended scope spans **operating-system foundations and compiler technology**.

The work is guided by the KISS philosophy: simple structures, explicit boundaries, and verification of actual behavior.

## Development goals

- **Real-time behavior:** make timing behavior an explicit design concern.
- **Predictability:** aim for behavior that can be understood and reasoned about.
- **Portability:** consider the ability to work across target environments.
- **Verifiability:** make design choices and resulting behavior open to inspection and testing.

These describe the direction of research and development. They do not assert that an implementation has achieved these properties.

## What is available here

This page introduces the vision and development goals. A public download, supported-platform matrix, benchmark, and dated release plan are not provided here.

> No delivered features, performance guarantees, or completion schedule are claimed. When implementation evidence is presented, it should identify the version, environment, method, result, and remaining limits.

## The relationship to Project-KISS

Project-KISS is the broader engineering philosophy. Project-AceIS is a product development initiative based on that philosophy. The product’s goals do not define the full scope of Project-KISS.
