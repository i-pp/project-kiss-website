## Website scope

This website introduces the Project-KISS philosophy, explains how it relates to Project-AceIS, and provides English and Japanese starting points for documentation and contribution.

The navigation separates philosophy, products, the AceIS vision, roadmap, documentation, and contribution guidance.

## Proposed documentation priorities

These are proposed next steps, not scheduled or completed product milestones.

1. **Document the foundations.** Keep the philosophy, governance, and product boundaries clear and consistent across both languages.
2. **Add reviewable engineering evidence.** Publish suitable design notes and verification reports with their methods, conditions, and limits after review.
3. **Expand product documentation when supported.** Add specifications, supported environments, and release information when there is evidence and a decision to publish them.

## AceIS release planning

The AceIS material presented on this site describes research and development goals. It does not establish a dated release plan, feature-completion status, or delivery commitment.

[Read the AceIS vision](/products/aceis/) for the stated goals. Proposed changes to this roadmap can be discussed through a [website issue](https://github.com/i-pp/project-kiss-website/issues).
